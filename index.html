Differential Geometry and Applications

Prof. Weiqing Gu
Fall 2026

Course Description

This course provides a rigorous introduction to the differential geometry of curves and surfaces and to the essential concept of manifolds—spaces constructed by patching together local coordinate systems. Students will study curvature, tangent spaces, metrics, geodesics, symmetry, and Lie groups, beginning with (S^1) and (SO(2)), then progressing to (SO(3)) and the rigid-motion group (SE(3)). Applications will include robotic motion and control, geometric methods in computer vision, and introductory quantum concepts involving (U(1)) and (SU(2)). The course will also explore how manifolds and geometric structures arise in the latent and representation spaces of generative AI. Students will gain both a rigorous mathematical foundation and an understanding of how differential geometry supports modern robotics, computer vision, quantum science, and artificial intelligence.
